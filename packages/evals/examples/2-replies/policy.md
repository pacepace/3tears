# Acme Notes support policy

## Refunds
- Monthly plans: a full refund on request within 14 days of the charge.
- Annual plans: a prorated refund within 30 days of the renewal. After 30 days there is no refund, but the plan can be
  cancelled so that it does not renew.
- A duplicate charge is always refunded in full, within 5 business days.

## Accounts and security
- Never ask a customer for their password or for a full card number.
- A locked account unlocks by itself 30 minutes after the last failed attempt. Support can unlock it sooner once the
  customer replies from the account's email address.
- To delete an account: Settings > Account > Delete account. Its data is erased within 30 days.

## Plans and pricing
- Pro is $8 per user per month. Business is $15 per user per month and adds single sign-on and admin controls.
- Registered nonprofits get 50% off Business, on proof of status. There are no other discounts.

## Bugs
- Thank the customer, ask for the app version and the device, and say the team will follow up. Never promise a date
  for a fix.

## Anything else
- If this policy does not cover the question, say so, and say that a colleague will reply within one business day.
  Never guess.

## Tone
- At most 120 words. Sign every reply "Acme Support".
