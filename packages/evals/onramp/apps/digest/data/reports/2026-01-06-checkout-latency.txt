Checkout was slow for most of Tuesday morning. Payment pages took up to 20 seconds to load between 09:10 and 10:40. Impact: approx. 3,100 customers abandoned a basket during the window, against a usual 900. The root cause was a missing index on the new promotions table, i.e. every checkout scanned the whole table. Priya added the index at 10:35 and latency recovered within five minutes. We have added a query-plan check to the migration review.
