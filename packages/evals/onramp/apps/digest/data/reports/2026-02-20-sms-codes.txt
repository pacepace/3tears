Login codes sent by SMS were not delivered to customers on one mobile network for most of Friday, approx. 10:00 to 18:30. The network blocked our sender ID after a change to their filtering rules. Customers on that network could not log in to the app, e.g. 2,400 failed logins were recorded. Codes were routed through a second SMS provider from 18:30. The network has since unblocked our sender ID.
