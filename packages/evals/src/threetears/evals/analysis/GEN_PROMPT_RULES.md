# Writer-prompt rule ledger

**For:** maintainers editing `gen_prompt.py` (`EVAL_ANALYSIS_GEN_DEFAULT`, the analysis writer's seed prompt).
**Answers:** what each numbered rule is there to prevent, so a rewrite does not drop a rule without knowing what
it cost to learn.

**How to use it.** The prompt is budgeted at `PROMPT_CHAR_BUDGET` (24,000 characters) and `PROMPT_RULE_BUDGET`
(13 rules), and both ceilings only fall; `tests/test_gen_prompt_budget.py` fails past either. A rule is a numbered
entry under the `RULES` heading; the paragraphs above it are not counted. A change that adds a rule names the rule
it replaces or absorbs, and edits this table in the same commit. A clause added inside a rule gets its reason added
to that row.

**The table is checked against the prompt.** `tests/test_gen_prompt_budget.py` reads each rule's number and
heading (its opening sentence, up to the first full stop, without it) from the prompt, and each row's `#` and
`Heading` from this table, and fails unless the two lists are equal. So a rule added without a row fails, a row
left behind for a deleted rule fails, and renumbering, reordering or rewording a heading fails until the row
moves with it.

**What a rule is.** Interpretation only (what to conclude, how firmly, in what order, what to recommend). Code owns
the shape (the schema sent as `response_format`), every number, and the report's first screen: its verdict line, its
scoreboard and both its charts. Whether a rule binds is measured by evaluating the
report writer, never by checking that a sentence is present.

## The rules

Reasons come from the private campaign history the engine was extracted from (summarised, not published) and from
this package's git log. "Reason not recorded" means no source states why; do not invent one.

| 1 | RANK ON TELEMETRY, NOT JUDGE SCORES | Rank on telemetry, not judge scores: goal checks first, latency on the mean with the tail beside it; a judged comparison stands only where `multiple_comparisons` separates it; an absolute judged claim needs a `calibrated` tier; quote bar `decision`s, never recompute them | A memo headlined "no quality was measured" over a fully judged campaign; memos written without one mechanical verdict | Judge bias mostly cancels across arms but not in an absolute claim, so comparisons are reportable before calibration and absolute claims are not. | 2 | A SHORTER TAIL CAN BE BOUGHT BY GIVING UP | Every latency claim carries the delivery rate for the same arms; `n_zero` is a proxy for declines, not a count | An arm bought a shorter tail by declining work, and quality was called "unhurt" while its failure rate went from 0% to 13% | A duration over delivered turns cannot see work that was never done |
| 3 | RANK ON THE SCOPE THAT MATCHES WHAT WAS TUNED | Rank on the scope that matches what was tuned; a scope divergence is its own finding that bounds the ranking one; check a model-call swing against provider throughput | A subsystem change ranked on whole-turn latency picked an arm whose subsystem had not moved and whose failures had risen; a ~205 s swing read as a lever effect was provider throughput drifting across sequential arms | A whole-run measure cannot attribute a movement to the part under test. | 4 | A LEVER THAT DID NOT MOVE THE METRIC AND A COMPARISON THAT COULD NOT RESOLVE ARE DIFFERENT FINDINGS | An established null and an unresolved comparison are different findings; no null from overlapping intervals; only `equivalent` says alike; `inert` / `unchecked` mechanisms qualify a null; a monotone ordering is weak evidence, never "no trend" | A 33% monotone reduction published as an absence, with a decision to stop tuning the lever | The two findings lead to opposite actions. | 5 | COUNT CASES, NOT ATTEMPTS | Count cases, not attempts: separation rests on `n_independent`; intervals are already over cases; an arm's planned `k` is not its delivered repeats | 5 cases × k=3 posed as 15 independent draws; a coverage floor of 1 read as "every arm ran once" while two arms ran k=3 | Repeats of one case are not independent draws (#590 made every interval cluster-aware, so the writer must not recompute over `n`). | 8 | STATE EVERY FINDING IN THE DIRECTION THE EVIDENCE SUPPORTS | State every finding in the direction the evidence supports | A refuted claim published at a low tier reads as support to a skimming reader | A low tier does not reverse a claim's direction |

| 11 | A DECISION IS ONE PROPOSAL AND A VERDICT ON IT | A decision is one positive proposal and a verdict; one choice is one decision; adopting a non-control arm needs an `improved` reading; never adopt over a `breached` guardrail; a deferral names a specific trigger; every decision names the findings it rests on; an exploratory campaign adopts nothing, keeping the control included; an unanswerable question says why, what would settle it and its rough cost | "Stop sweeping X" filed under Rejected; "Adopt A" beside "Reject B" for one choice; generic revisit triggers; an arm marked adopted that no comparison had separated from the control; "Keep the current model — adopted" in a campaign that declared no question, which the arm table then printed as the control being the "winner" (G2, 2026-10) | Each is a decision a reader cannot act on cleanly. The exploratory and `rests_on` clauses are also refused by the generator, structurally (`.26`) |
| 12 | NEXT EXPERIMENTS ARE ORDERED BY LEVERAGE, AND LEVERAGE IS ABOUT A DECISION | Next experiments are ordered by leverage, and leverage is about a decision this analysis records | The top-ranked next step was the run that would reopen the decision the memo had just adopted | An experiment that could overturn an adopted decision shows that decision was really deferred |

## Not rules, but load-bearing

- **The per-sentence pruning test** (preamble, under "WRITE LESS"): every sentence must change what the reader, who
  decides what to change next, does or believes. Rule 13's cut applies it. Stated once as the general test because
  a list of things to cut passes every sentence of a kind the list does not name (pacepace/3tears#682).
- **"Every number a reader sees is code's"** (preamble; formerly a rule). It prevents fabricated citations and
  hand-typed figures nobody could find in the evidence. A finding that cites no reading has empty evidence.
- **The figure-reference grammar is appended by the generator** (`prose_refs.reference_grammar`), written from the
  parser's constants. A copy kept in a stored prompt went on teaching each deployment the grammar it was seeded
  with after the parser changed, and every generation there was refused at full cost.
- **No worked WRONG/RIGHT examples.** A model reproduced a prompt's canonical answer with its nouns swapped.
