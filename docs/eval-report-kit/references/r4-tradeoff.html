Full results

Every arm and aspect
Test set
300 conversations replayed from September logs, 2,400 assistant replies. Every arm answered the same user turns, so differences are paired.
In-character rate
LLM judge with the persona rubric, calibrated on 240 human-rated replies (agreement 0.82). This is the only judged aspect that moved.
Guardrails
Standing policy, not set for this campaign: declines to speculate at least 95%, music facts correct at least 90%. Options that break one are shown but left out of the pricing unless you include them.
Intervals
95% intervals on paired differences. Not corrected across the three challengers.
Hybrid
Modeled, not run: A serves turns up to the handoff, Production serves the rest. Cost and in-character rate are weighted by the share of replies at each depth.
Cost
Measured tokens times list price per 1,000 replies, at 12 million replies a month. Later turns cost more because the context is longer.
